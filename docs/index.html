1Overview and objectives

Treatment decisions for lumbar spinal stenosis (LSS) are uncertain, and most existing prognostic models were developed in surgical cohorts. Using the PROSPECTS cohort, this 12-week project will develop and internally validate models that predict 12-month outcomes in adults aged 50 and over who are starting non-surgical care, as specified in the Statistical Analysis Plan (SAP). Three outcomes will be modelled separately: Swiss Spinal Stenosis Questionnaire (SSSQ) symptom severity, Oswestry Disability Index (ODI) and Global Rating of Change (GRC).

Success criteria

  • Complete all analyses specified in the SAP for the three outcomes.
  • Report validation performance with 95% confidence intervals.
  • Make the full analysis reproducible, from raw data to final tables.
  • Complete the final report and the required TRIPOD+AI and STROBE checklists by week 12.

Assumptions and constraints

  • Three members, about 10 hours per member per week.
  • Data are available in week 1 (development n = 443; validation n = 111).
  • Additional predictors will be confirmed with Dr. Rundell.
  • BI-SS and the optimism-corrected bootstrap (200 replicates) may require substantial computing time.

2Scope

In scope: descriptive analyses, multiple imputation, BI-SS group LASSO with ridge refit, internal validation, bootstrap optimism correction, sensitivity analyses, tables, figures and the final report.

Out of scope: new data collection; external validation; surgical outcomes; a clinical decision tool; alternative primary modelling approaches; and outcomes other than SSSQ, ODI and GRC.

Dependencies

The primary models depend on the analysis dataset and imputation pipeline. Internal validation uses the completed primary-model pipeline and the prespecified development and validation samples. Sensitivity analyses and final tables depend on the validation results.

5Resources

  • Software: R (mice, grpreg, glmnet, ranger, xgboost, rms, pROC) and a shared code repository.
  • Computing and budget: UW computing cluster for the bootstrap steps; no direct costs.

6Quality management

  • Each script is reviewed by a second member before its results are used.
  • Fixed random seeds are used and package versions are recorded.
  • Imputation convergence is checked with trace plots.
  • The validation procedure is fixed before modelling, and validation results are not used for tuning or model selection.
  • Deviations from the SAP are logged and reported.

9Change control and closure

Any proposed change to scope, schedule or the SAP is submitted in writing and reviewed at the weekly meeting. Changes that affect the SAP also require the instructor's approval. Every change is logged with its date and reason.

At closure, the team reviews the final report and checklists, archives the code and session information, and holds a short lessons-learned meeting.
